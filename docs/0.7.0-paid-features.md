# 0.7.0 paid features

Checklist for Pandora Plus and Pandora Premium. Nothing here is built. Every
API still needs a live capture once a paid account is available. Offline
downloads are a mobile-app feature and are out of scope.

Free accounts keep working. Paid actions stay hidden or labeled until the
account's tier says they are allowed.

## Pandora Plus

- [ ] No audio ads: turn off ad handling and muted ads.
- [ ] Unlimited skips: hide the skip counter, or show unlimited.
- [ ] Replay a song: check `adSupportedReplay` / `allowReplay`.
- [ ] Higher quality, 192 kbps: retry the MP3 request that comes back HTTP 400.
- [ ] Unlimited personalized stations.

## Pandora Premium

- [ ] On-demand search and play of songs, albums, and artists.
- [ ] Playlists: create, edit, and play, plus Pandora's auto playlists.
- [ ] Play thumbed songs on demand from the all-thumbs list.
- [ ] Collection: add, and play on demand.
- [ ] Full album pages, playable in order.
- [ ] Podcast extras, if a paid account has any the free app does not.

## App work

- [ ] Detect the tier from login / the account, and gate features so a free account still works.
- [ ] Show the tier in Settings and on the status bar.
- [ ] Hide or label paid-only actions while the account is free.

## Notes

- [ ] Capture each API live before building it. A paid Pandora account is required.
- [ ] Leave offline downloads out. They belong to the mobile app.
